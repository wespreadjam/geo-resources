# papers

## read this one

**geo: generative engine optimization** — aggarwal et al, kdd 2024.
[arxiv 2311.09735](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735) · [princeton page](https://collaborate.princeton.edu/en/publications/geo-generative-engine-optimization/)

- introduces geo-bench (~10k queries, 9 domains)
- targeted tactics: +22–41% citations
- strongest: **statistics addition, cite sources, quotation addition**
- weakest: keyword stuffing, fluency rewrites

## related
