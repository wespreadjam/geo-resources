# what is geo

make llms cite you.

## three metrics

1. presence — are you in the answer
2. citation — is your url linked
3. accuracy — is the claim about you correct

a wrong mention is worse than no mention.

## origin

term coined by aggarwal et al, kdd 2024. [arxiv 2311.09735](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735). also called aeo or llmo. same thing.

## what changed

- ai answers replaced link lists
- 60% of google searches = no click
- coding agents read docs, pick packages
- discovery + evaluation + install collapse into one chat turn

## what didn't

- authority wins
- primary sources matter
- technical correctness is the floor
